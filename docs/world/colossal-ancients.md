# Colossal Ancients

The Colossal Ancients are Talic, Madawc and Korlic's colossal forms, fought in Hell by Reign of the Warlock characters. Put the five Colossal Ancient statues in the [Horadric Cube](../items/cube-recipes.md) to open a red portal to them (area [Colossal Summit](act-5/colossal-summit.md)).

## The statues

Each act boss in Hell can drop a statue, most often its own. Chance per kill, one player:

| Boss | Statue | Chance | Terrorized | Any statue | Any statue, terrorized |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Andariel | Talic's Anguish | 1 in 23 | 1 in 15 | 1 in 20 | 1 in 14 |
| Duriel | Korlic's Pain | 1 in 29 | 1 in 15 | 1 in 26 | 1 in 13 |
| Mephisto | Madawc's Ire | 1 in 28 | 1 in 14 | 1 in 25 | 1 in 13 |
| Diablo | Bul-Kathos' Nightmare | 1 in 28 | 1 in 14 | 1 in 25 | 1 in 13 |
| Baal | Worusk's End | 1 in 28 | 1 in 14 | 1 in 25 | 1 in 13 |

"Terrorized" covers the boss's terrorized treasure classes; the game picks one of them, so it's shown as a range.

## The fight

From Maxroll's guide, in short:

- All three fight at once. Each has its own element: Talic fire, Korlic cold, Madawc lightning.
  Each has a Conviction-like aura that lowers your resistance to its element by 100, so
  over-capped resistances matter. Thawing Potions help against Korlic.
- Each summons up to five Protector Spirit minions, which are Extra Fast and immune to their
  Ancient's element. Killing an Ancient quickly releases the rest of its minions at once.
- The Ancients enrage at 50% and 25% life, and when another of them dies. After the first one
  dies the others gain new attacks (Talic's Whirlwind leaves fire twisters; Korlic's Leap ends
  in a cold fissure; Madawc's throws release Charged Bolts), and after the second, more (a
  volcano, Blizzard, a thunderstorm).
- The jewel you get depends on which Ancient dies last. Only one Colossal jewel works per
  character.

Blizzard's 3.2 patch notes raised the Ancients' magic resistance from 50 to 75 and let statues
drop from act bosses outside terror zones (the table odds above include both).
