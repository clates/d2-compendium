| Required level | 57 (the highest rune's) |

## Properties

- +1 to All Skills
- +40% Increased Attack Speed
- +(250-290)% Enhanced Damage
- Adds 5-30 fire damage
- 7% Life stolen per hit
- +(2-6) to Battle Command
- +(1-6) to Battle Orders
- +(1-4) to Battle Cry
- Prevent Monster Heal
- Replenish Life +12
- 30% Better Chance of Getting Magic Items

The list includes the runes' own socket bonuses. The base must be a normal, superior or low-quality item (not magic, rare, set or unique) with exactly 5 empty sockets, and the runes go in in the order above. See [Sockets and runewords](../../mechanics/sockets-and-runewords.md).
