[Tir](../runes.md#tir-rune) + [Ith](../runes.md#ith-rune) + [Sol](../runes.md#sol-rune) + [Ko](../runes.md#ko-rune) in a 4-socket missile weapon.

| --- | --- |

| Required level | 39 (the highest rune's) |
| Fits | Missile Weapon |

## Properties

- Level 10 Vigor Aura When Equipped
- +(200-275)% Enhanced Damage
- +9 to Minimum Damage
- Adds 55-160 fire damage
- Adds 55-160 lightning damage
- Adds 55-160 cold damage
- +(2-6) to Valkyrie
- +10 to Dexterity
- Regenerate Mana 20%
- +2 to Mana after each Kill
- +2 to Light Radius
- Level 20 Revive (25/25 Charges)

The list includes the runes' own socket bonuses. The base must be a normal, superior or low-quality item (not magic, rare, set or unique) with exactly 4 empty sockets, and the runes go in in the order above. See [Sockets and runewords](../../mechanics/sockets-and-runewords.md).
