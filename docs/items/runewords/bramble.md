# Bramble

[Ral](../runes.md#ral-rune) + [Ohm](../runes.md#ohm-rune) + [Sur](../runes.md#sur-rune) + [Eth](../runes.md#eth-rune) in a 4-socket armor.

| --- | --- |

| Required level | 61 (the highest rune's) |

## Properties

- Level (15-21) Thorns Aura When Equipped
- +50% Faster Hit Recovery
- +(25-50)% to Poison Skill Damage
- +300 Defense
- Increase Maximum Mana 5%
- Regenerate Mana 15%
- +5% to Maximum Cold Resist
- Fire Resist +30%
- Poison Resist +100%
- +13 Life after each Kill
- Level 13 Spirit of Barbs (33/33 Charges)

The list includes the runes' own socket bonuses. The base must be a normal, superior or low-quality item (not magic, rare, set or unique) with exactly 4 empty sockets, and the runes go in in the order above. See [Sockets and runewords](../../mechanics/sockets-and-runewords.md).
