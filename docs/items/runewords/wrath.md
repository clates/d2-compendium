## Properties

- 30% Chance to cast level 1 Decrepify on striking
- 5% Chance to cast level 10 Life Tap on striking
- +375% Damage to Demons
- +100 to Attack Rating against Demons
- +(250-300)% Damage to Undead
- Adds 85-120 magic damage
- Adds 41-240 lightning damage
- +20% Chance of Crushing Blow
- Prevent Monster Heal
- +10 to Energy
- Cannot Be Frozen

The list includes the runes' own socket bonuses. The base must be a normal, superior or low-quality item (not magic, rare, set or unique) with exactly 4 empty sockets, and the runes go in in the order above. See [Sockets and runewords](../../mechanics/sockets-and-runewords.md).
