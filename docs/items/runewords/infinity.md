# Infinity

[Ber](../runes.md#ber-rune) + [Mal](../runes.md#mal-rune) + [Ber](../runes.md#ber-rune) + [Ist](../runes.md#ist-rune) in a 4-socket polearm, spear.

| Required level | 63 (the highest rune's) |

## Properties

- 50% Chance to cast level 20 Chain Lightning when you Kill an Enemy
- Level 12 Conviction Aura When Equipped
- +35% Faster Run/Walk
- +(255-325)% Enhanced Damage
- -(45-55)% to Enemy Lightning Resistance
- +40% Chance of Crushing Blow
- Prevent Monster Heal
- +0.5 to Vitality per Character Level
- 30% Better Chance of Getting Magic Items
- Level 21 Cyclone Armor (30/30 Charges)

The list includes the runes' own socket bonuses. The base must be a normal, superior or low-quality item (not magic, rare, set or unique) with exactly 4 empty sockets, and the runes go in in the order above. See [Sockets and runewords](../../mechanics/sockets-and-runewords.md).
