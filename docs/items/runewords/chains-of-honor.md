[Dol](../runes.md#dol-rune) + [Um](../runes.md#um-rune) + [Ber](../runes.md#ber-rune) + [Ist](../runes.md#ist-rune) in a 4-socket armor.

## Properties

- +2 to All Skills
- +200% Damage to Demons
- +100% Damage to Undead
- 8% Life stolen per hit
- +70% Enhanced Defense
- +20 to Strength
- Replenish Life +7
- All Resistances +65
- Physical Damage Received Reduced by 8%
- 25% Better Chance of Getting Magic Items

The list includes the runes' own socket bonuses. The base must be a normal, superior or low-quality item (not magic, rare, set or unique) with exactly 4 empty sockets, and the runes go in in the order above. See [Sockets and runewords](../../mechanics/sockets-and-runewords.md).
