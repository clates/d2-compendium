# Quests

Each act has six quests (Act 4 has three). Every difficulty has its own set, so each reward can
be earned three times. Quests that only open the way forward have no reward beyond that.

## Rewards worth having

Per difficulty, the permanent rewards add up to 4 skill points, 5 stat points, 20 life and +10 to
all resistances:

| Quest | Act | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Den of Evil | 1 | +1 skill point, and Akara resets your skills and stats once |
| Radament's Lair | 2 | +1 skill point (Book of Skill); Act 2 prices 10% lower |
| The Golden Bird | 3 | +20 life (Potion of Life) |
| Lam Esen's Tome | 3 | +5 stat points |
| The Fallen Angel | 4 | +2 skill points |
| Prison of Ice | 5 | +10 to all resistances (Scroll of Resistance) |

Item and service rewards:

| Quest | Act | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Tools of the Trade | 1 | Charsi imbues one normal item into a random rare, at item level character level + 4 (Maxroll), so saving it for a high-level character and a good base pays off |
| The Hellforge | 4 | gems (one normal, two flawless, one perfect) and a random rune: El to Amn in Normal (Maxroll says El to Sol), Sol to Um in Nightmare, Hel to Gul in Hell |
| Siege on Harrogath | 5 | Larzuk adds sockets to one unsocketed item: the base's maximum for a plain item, 1 or 2 for a magic one, 1 for rare, set, unique and crafted ones. See [Sockets and runewords](../mechanics/sockets-and-runewords.md) |
| Rescue on Mount Arreat | 5 | Qual-Kehk gives three runes: Ral, Ort and Tal |
| Prison of Ice | 5 | Malah's scroll above, and a rare item for the character's class |
| Betrayal of Harrogath | 5 | Anya personalizes one weapon or armor: adds your name, repairs and recharges it |
| Rite of Passage | 5 | a fixed amount of experience, at most one level's worth |

> **Unverified:** the smaller rewards not listed above. The Arreat Summit's act pages don't give
> them: Kashya's free mercenary (Sisters' Burial Grounds), Cain identifying items for free (Search
> for Cain), and a free mercenary from Asheara (Blade of the Old Religion). The Rite of Passage
> needs character level 20, 40 or 60 by difficulty. Its experience is 1.4 million in Normal,
> 20 million in Nightmare and 40 million in Hell, per the Arreat Summit's Lord of Destruction pages.
> These are older pages; Reign of the Warlock may have changed some rewards.
