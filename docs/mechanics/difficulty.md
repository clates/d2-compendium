# Difficulty

Each difficulty unlocks after beating the previous one. The numbers are in
[Difficulty levels](../reference/difficulty.md); this page explains them.

## Resistance penalty

A character's resistances are lowered by 40 in Nightmare and 100 in Hell (20 and 50 for Classic
characters). Resistances are capped at 75% (more with "+% to maximum resist" items), and the
cap applies after the penalty, so Hell needs 175 resistance from gear to stay at 75.

## Monster changes

- Monsters are the area level ([Monster level](monster-level.md)), so they're stronger and drop
  better items.
- They gain resistances and immunities: compare a monster's rows on its page.
- Monsters' skills get the "Monster skill level bonus" (+3 in Nightmare, +7 in Hell).
- Static Field can't take a monster below 33% of its life in Nightmare or 50% in Hell.

## Weaker effects on monsters

The divisors divide an effect's strength or length: in Hell, life and mana steal work at a third
of their Normal strength, and cold and freeze last a quarter as long on monsters.

> **Unverified:** what the AI curse divisor applies to (curses cast by monsters, or on them).

## Bosses, mercenaries and minions

- Mercenaries deal less damage to bosses in later difficulties: 50% in Normal, 35% in Nightmare,
  25% in Hell.
- Monsters the game flags as Prime Evils (all five act bosses, their Uber versions, Diablo
  Clone and the Colossal Summit bosses) deal double damage to mercenaries and four times the
  damage to minions (summons), in every difficulty.
- Players deal 17% of their damage to other players.

## Gambling

Gambled items are rare 10% of the time, set 0.1% and unique 0.05% (out of 100,000: 10,000, 100
and 50). The exceptional and elite factors feed a formula with the character's level.

> **Unverified:** the exceptional and elite gamble formula isn't documented here yet.
