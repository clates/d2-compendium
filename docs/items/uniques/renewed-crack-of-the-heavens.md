It's made in the [Horadric Cube](../cube-recipes.md): 1 Fal Rune + 1 Perfect Topaz + 1 Uber Ancient Summon Material Act3 + 1 Crack of the Heavens -> Magnetic Crack of the Heavens. The unique that goes in is [Latent Crack of the Heavens](latent-crack-of-the-heavens.md); the recipe text uses older names.
