## Where it comes from

This unique never rolls when a Grand Charm drops as a unique (`spawnable` is off). It's made in the [Horadric Cube](../cube-recipes.md): 1 Lum Rune + 1 Perfect Sapphire + 1 Uber Ancient Summon Material Act2 + 1 Cold Rupture -> Gelid Cold Rupture. The unique that goes in is [Latent Cold Rupture](latent-cold-rupture.md); the recipe text uses older names.
