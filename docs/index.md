## Reading the pages

- Generated pages say `generated: true` at the top and record the game version they came from.
  Anything below the NOTES line at the bottom is hand-written and survives regeneration.
- **†** marks content that exists only for Reign of the Warlock characters.
- Ranges like `+(30-50)%` are the roll range; a fixed number doesn't roll.
- **Unverified** boxes mark claims that come from the community or the game's code rather than
  its tables, and haven't been checked in game for this version.
