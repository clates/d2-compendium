## Where it comes from

This unique never rolls when a Grand Charm drops as a unique (`spawnable` is off). It's made in the [Horadric Cube](../cube-recipes.md): 1 Io Rune + 1 Perfect Ruby + 1 Uber Ancient Summon Material Act4 + 1 Flame Rift -> Incendiary Flame Rift. The unique that goes in is [Latent Flame Rift](latent-flame-rift.md); the recipe text uses older names.
